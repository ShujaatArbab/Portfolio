Case Study Summary

The Challenge & The Outcome

What Rasant Solutions needed before this platform existed — and what changed once it shipped.

The Problem

Rasant Solutions needed a centralized system to manage core HR and business operations — payroll, attendance, documents, hiring, credentials, and support inquiries — which were scattered across manual processes and disconnected tools. Team communication also lacked a real-time solution, slowing down internal coordination and making it hard for leadership to get a clear, live picture of the business.

The Outcome

Rasant Solutions now runs payroll, attendance, leave, documents, hiring, credential sharing, inquiries, and internal communication through a single unified, role-based platform. Manual, fragmented workflows were eliminated, replaced with an automated increment/payroll engine, a full applicant tracking pipeline, encrypted credential sharing, SSE-powered real-time messaging, and bi-directional Jira/worklog tracking — giving the team live visibility into every part of the business for the first time.

Engineering Problem Solving

Key Challenges & Technical Solutions

Real architectural bottlenecks solved during the development lifecycle.

01 · Real-Time Communication

Challenge: Traditional polling caused high database load and excessive HTTP handshakes, while full WebSockets added unnecessary infrastructure complexity for one-way alert streaming. SSE reconnection also failed after logout/login cycles due to component caching.

Solution: Implemented Server-Sent Events via Django streaming HTTP responses, added a `mark_offline` endpoint, fixed `` caching of the Inbox component, and built client-side auto-reconnection logic with Pinia message caching for zero-latency updates.

02 · Financial Data Integrity

Challenge: Concurrent adjustments to payroll variables (increments, bonuses, leave deductions, tax brackets) risked race conditions and dirty writes during batch disbursement.

Solution: Enforced atomic database transactions with row-level locks on contract tables in MySQL, plus per-row atomic savepoints for bulk attendance uploads, ensuring calculation consistency before slip generation.

03 · Cross-Origin & Token Expiration

Challenge: Token expiration during long administrator shifts caused API call drops and data loss in unfinished form submissions, including `request.user` resolving as `AnonymousUser` inside SSE streams.

Solution: Configured Axios response interceptors with automatic JWT silent refresh mechanisms that queue pending requests and replay them seamlessly upon token renewal, and fixed authentication context propagation into SSE streams.

04 · Async Race Conditions & UI Consistency

Challenge: Rapid CV submission polling and status dropdown interactions risked stale data overwrites, while `overflow-x-auto` tables clipped dropdown z-index.

Solution: Added a `cvRequestId` token guard to prevent race conditions, Teleport-based status dropdowns to escape table clipping, and a reusable `usePagedList()` composable factory across list-heavy modules.
