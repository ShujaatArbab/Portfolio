Case Study Summary

The Challenge & The Outcome

What sparked LPG Booking App — and what shipped as a working cross-platform product.

The Problem

LPG cylinder booking and delivery is often managed through phone calls and manual processes, making it difficult for customers to check availability, compare rates, track orders, or know when their cylinders will be delivered. I set out to build a complete LPG booking and distribution system that simplifies the process for customers, vendors, and suppliers while providing better order, inventory, and delivery management.

The Outcome

The LPG Booking & Delivery System was developed as a complete role-based application where customers can book cylinders, select vendors and cylinder types, track orders, and manage their profiles. Vendors can process customer orders, manage deliveries, and place orders with suppliers, while suppliers manage inventory and distribute cylinders across shops — all supported by a REST API, SQL database, and efficient order and inventory management.

Engineering Problem Solving

Key Challenges & Technical Solutions

Technical challenges addressed throughout the development of the LPG booking, distribution, and inventory management system.

01 · Role-Based Access

Challenge: Different users required different permissions and workflows across the customer, vendor, and supplier portals.

Solution: Implemented role-based access control to provide each user type with the appropriate dashboards, features, and operations.

02 · Inventory Synchronization

Challenge: Keeping cylinder stock accurate across multiple vendor shops and supplier plants required consistent inventory updates.

Solution: Connected inventory operations with the backend database to update stock records as cylinders were ordered, dispatched, and delivered.

03 · Order Workflow Management

Challenge: Orders move through multiple stages from customer booking to vendor processing and final delivery.

Solution: Designed structured order statuses and REST API workflows to track orders throughout the complete LPG distribution process.

04 · Multi-Level Supply Management

Challenge: Managing the relationship between customers, vendors, shops, suppliers, and plants created a complex distribution workflow.

Solution: Built separate vendor and supplier dashboards with centralized order and inventory management to simplify the overall supply chain.
